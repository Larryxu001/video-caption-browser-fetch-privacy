Privacy policy

Video Caption Browser Fetch · Larry Xu · Updated October 1, 2026

This policy describes the Microsoft Edge browser extension. It captures video captions and exports Markdown notes directly to the Obsidian desktop app. It has no developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising service.

Data used

When you request capture, the extension uses the selected video URL, title, channel, available metadata and captions. Requests to a video platform may use the browser’s existing signed-in session. The extension does not export browser cookies to the developer or recognition providers.

Optional speech recognition

Recognition is off by default. Enabling it and saving your settings authorizes the extension to download audio for requested videos without captions and send it over HTTPS to your selected Groq or OpenAI service with your API key. The service processes the audio and returns transcript text. Charges may apply. The developer does not receive your key, audio or transcript. Provider retention and use are governed by that provider’s terms and your account configuration; this extension cannot promise zero retention or delete data already received by the provider.

Optional Douyin page capture

Off by default. If enabled, a script on Douyin pages observes video responses while you browse, even before requesting an export. It retains up to 600 video records, including caption and media addresses, in the page’s memory. This cache is not sent to the developer. Save and reload all open Douyin pages to apply disabling or clear existing page caches. A recognition upload still requires enabled recognition and an explicit export request.

Local storage and clipboard

Settings, vault/folder choices and API keys are stored in the Edge profile using local extension storage, without additional extension-level encryption. Keys are not injected into video pages. Completed recognition parts may be stored for retry, bounded to three checkpoints; checkpoints older than seven days are not reused, but may remain stored until cleared or replaced. Audio is processed in memory, not intentionally saved as audio files.

Export replaces the system clipboard with the finished Markdown note, then uses the Obsidian URL scheme to create a note. Clipboard fallback may temporarily place the finished note in the selected video page’s DOM. Other software with clipboard access, and scripts on that page, may be able to read it. Avoid exporting sensitive transcripts on untrusted pages. Saved notes remain in the destination vault and follow that app’s sync and backup settings.

Your controls

You can disable recognition and Douyin capture in options, remove saved API keys, and clear saved recognition progress. Stop active tasks by closing their video tabs or disabling the extension before clearing data; active requests may finish remotely and active tasks may recreate checkpoints. Removing keys does not revoke them at the provider. Uninstalling removes local extension storage; it does not remove exported notes, clipboard history, backups or provider-held data.

Sharing and changes

Data is used for the capture, transcription and export features described here. It is not sold or used for advertising. Video platforms, your chosen recognition provider and the destination app have their own policies. This policy will be updated when the extension’s data practices change.
